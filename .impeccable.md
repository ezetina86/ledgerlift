## Design Context

### Users
Single user — Enrique. Personal fitness tracker used on Pixel 9 between sets in the gym. One-handed, glanced at under fluorescent lights, used while mentally fatigued from effort. Every interaction must be instant and readable at arm's length.

### Brand Personality
Bold · Athletic · Technical

Not a wellness app. Not a social fitness app. A precision instrument for serious training. Feels like competition timing equipment — a Garmin race computer, a powerlifting scoreboard, an F1 telemetry readout. Data is the hero. Every screen exists to serve the lift.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark (mandatory — gym lighting + OLED Pixel 9 battery).
Purple accent (#863bff range) — distinctive, not the expected "gym green." Builds on the favicon identity.
Typography: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold for all metric numbers and headings. Barlow Regular/Medium for body copy. Condensed bold numerics make weights and reps read like a scoreboard from 60cm away.
Palette: OKLCH-based, near-black surfaces with subtle purple tint (chroma 0.008–0.012). Single vivid purple accent at oklch(62% 0.24 293).

**The unforgettable thing**: when you open the set logger mid-workout, the weight and reps are displayed in 72px+ condensed bold — like a competition scoreboard. Instantly legible, unmistakably purposeful.

### Anti-References
- Generic "fitness app" teal + white (MyFitnessPal, Google Fit)
- Motivational gradient text and glow cards
- Wellness/meditation softness (Calm, Whoop consumer UX)
- SaaS dashboard cards with border-left stripes

### Design Principles
1. **Numbers are primary.** Weight, reps, volume — these are the content. Typography serves them first.
2. **Minimum viable chrome.** Every UI element must justify its existence. No decorative elements.
3. **Readability under load.** High contrast, large touch targets, no tiny interactive elements.
4. **Precision over warmth.** Clinical accuracy feels respectful to serious training, not cold.
5. **Dark is non-negotiable.** OLED screen, dim gym environment, preserve night vision between sets.
